    Paralleling Trainer – Student Guide

    Phase 1: Black Start & Dead Bus Closing

    1. Click Start on DG 1. The diesel engine fires up at 10% fuel and 20% field (nominal 60.00 Hz / 6.60 kV).
    2. Notice the Bus A & Bus B indicators at the bottom are DEAD (grey).
    3. Click Close CB on DG 1. Because the busbar is completely dead, the breaker closes instantly with no synchroscope check required (Dead Bus Incomer Permissive).
    4. The busbar turns ENERGIZED RED.
    5. Move the Load demand slider up to 1,500 kW. Notice DG 1 speed droops slightly. Raise Fuel on DG 1 to recover 60.00 Hz, or simply press Auto!

    Phase 2: Synchronizing Incoming Unit (DG 2)

    1. Click Start on DG 2. Select DG 2 in the top of the Synchronising panel.
    2. Look at the Synchroscope: The needle is rotating!
      • If turning counter-clockwise (Slow direction), DG 2 is running slower than the bus.
      • Use the Fuel knob (governor) to raise DG 2 speed until the needle turns slowly clockwise (Fast direction, ~1 turn every 4–5 seconds).
    3. Trim the Field knob (AVR) until the voltage matches the bus within ±4.8%.
    4. When both Δ Frequency and Δ Voltage show green checkmarks, click Close CB.
    5. The breaker is now ARMED. The check-sync relay automatically closes the breaker at exact 12 o'clock (phase angle ≤ ±2°)!

    Phase 3: Load Sharing & Reverse Power (ANSI 32R)

    1. Immediately after paralleling, DG 2 is carrying ~0 kW while DG 1 carries the ship load.
    2. To share load manually, increase Fuel on DG 2. You will see kW transfer from DG 1 to DG 2!
    3. Warning – Reverse Power Trip: If you drop Fuel too low on a closed generator, active power drops below -4%. An alarm sounds with a 10-second countdown! If not corrected, the breaker trips to protect the prime mover.
    4. Click Auto on all running generators for automatic isochronous equal load sharing.

    Marine Switchboard Architecture

    Modern commercial vessels and electric propulsion ships use split medium-voltage switchboards (IEC 60092 / SOLAS):

    • BUS SECTION A (Port Switchboard): Fed by DG 1 and DG 2.
    • BUS SECTION B (Starboard Switchboard): Fed by DG 3 and DG 4.
    • BUS TIE (BT-1 / ANSI 52BT): Center Vacuum Circuit Breaker linking Section A and Section B.

    Split-Bus vs. Tied-Bus Operation

    1. Click the center [ BUS TIE: CLOSED ] button to open the tie breaker.
    2. The switchboard is now in Split Bus Mode (redundant DP mode):
      • Bus A and Bus B are electrically isolated.
      • DG 1 & DG 2 supply Port Bus A.
      • DG 3 & DG 4 supply Starboard Bus B.
    3. To close the Bus Tie when both sections are energized, the two independent buses must be in synchronism! If not, the tie breaker interlock inhibits closure.

    Breaker Cubicle (Drawout VCB)

    Each generator bay features an authentic medium-voltage vacuum circuit breaker (VCB) cradle with upper/lower isolating disconnect bushings, 3× Current Transformers, Earth Switch (ES), and multifunction protection relay (ANSI 25 Sync, 32R Reverse Power, 50/51 Overcurrent, 27/59 Voltage).
